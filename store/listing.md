## Full description (max 4000)

Tengen is a clean, free app to play Go — also called Baduk or Weiqi — on online-go.com (OGS),
the largest free Go server, and on your phone without internet.

PLAY
• Find an opponent near your rank in one tap: pick the board (9×9, 13×13, 19×19) and the speed
  (blitz, rapid, live or daily correspondence)
• Play the KataGo bots of OGS, from beginner to strong dan levels
• Play offline against a beginner bot, or pass and play with a friend on one phone
• Japanese, Chinese, Korean and AGA rules, handicaps, Fischer and byo-yomi clocks
• Counting at the end of the game: tap the dead stones, then accept the score

YOUR GAMES
• All your ongoing games, the ones waiting for your move first
• Your rank, rating, record and finished games
• Chat with your opponent

LEARN
• The OGS Learning Hub course, from the rules to beginner levels
• Puzzle collections: life and death, tesuji and more, with hints
• Coordinate training against the clock

WATCH
• Top live and correspondence games on OGS, with their boards

AND
• Beautiful boards and stones from OGS (kaya, walnut, slate and shell, and more), stone sounds
• Dark and light themes
• Free software (GPL-3.0), no ads, no tracking

Tengen uses the public online-go.com API. It is not made by, or affiliated with, OGS. An
online-go.com account is needed to play online; you can create one for free on online-go.com.
