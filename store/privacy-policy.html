Tengen privacy policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Tengen is a free, open source app to play Go on online-go.com (OGS) and on your phone. This page explains what data the app handles. In short: Tengen has no servers of its own, shows no ads, and does not collect analytics or track you.

Data sent to online-go.com

When you use the online features, the app talks directly to online-go.com, the Go server run by the OGS team, as their own website does:

  • Sign-in. Your OGS username and password are sent to online-go.com to sign you in. The app does not keep your password.
  • Playing and chatting. Your moves, game requests and chat messages are sent to online-go.com, where other players can see them as on the website.
  • Browsing. Puzzles, lessons from the Learning Hub, player profiles and the games you watch are loaded from online-go.com.

What online-go.com does with this data is described in the OGS privacy policy. Tengen is not made by, or affiliated with, OGS.

Data kept on your phone

  • Your OGS session (the cookies online-go.com gives after sign-in), in the phone's encrypted storage, so you stay signed in. Signing out deletes it.
  • Your settings and progress: board and stones, sound, the last puzzle, the lessons you finished, coordinate training scores and offline games.

This data stays on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What Tengen does not do

  • No servers of its own: nothing is sent to the developer.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking.
  • No sale or sharing of data with anyone.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Children

Tengen is not directed at children under 13. Playing online requires an online-go.com account, under the terms of online-go.com.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above changed. Questions can be asked in the project's issues on GitHub: github.com/prathpatel/tengen/issues.
